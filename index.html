Read, Learn and Connect

Nnoo! Welcome to Awka Community Library.

Awka Community Library is a free library for everyone in the city. we lend books and magazines, offer quiet study desks and run a computer lab for learners of all ages.

The library opened in 2015 and is run by volunteers from the Awka Reading Society (ARS) more than 4,000 members visit us every year.
